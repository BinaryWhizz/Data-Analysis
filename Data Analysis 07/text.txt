Marketing & Sales Revenue Dataset

This synthetic dataset was designed to help machine learning students and practitioners build, train, and evaluate 
regression models in a realistic marketing and sales context. It simulates transactional and behavioral data from a 
multi-region retail/e-commerce business operating across the Middle East and North Africa.

The dataset contains 60,000 rows and 22 features, covering marketing spend, customer behavior, digital engagement, 
and competitive pricing — all influencing a single continuous target variable: sales revenue in USD.
