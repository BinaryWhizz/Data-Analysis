AI Evolution Intelligence Dataset

This dataset presents a structured timeline and analytical record of modern conversational AI development.
It includes model releases, adoption growth, feature popularity, efficiency, accuracy and market influence.

The dataset helps researchers and students understand how intelligent systems evolved from basic chat models into 
powerful multimodal assistants.

Purpose:
To analyze growth, capability improvements, and real-world impact of conversational artificial intelligence systems 
over time.


Key Highlights:

Model release history

User growth statistics

Feature usage trends

Performance improvements

Accuracy benchmarks

Market influence metrics
