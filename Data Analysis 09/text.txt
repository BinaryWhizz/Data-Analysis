Spotify Global Trends & Popularity Analysis
Detailed Analysis of Worldwide Track Performance and Artist Popularity

Dataset Overview
This dataset provides a comprehensive numerical and categorical summary of the modern music streaming landscape. 
Its primary objective is to track market shifts, measure the popularity of specific tracks, and analyze the global 
reach of various artists.

Data Set Metrics:
Each entry in the dataset represents a specific track and includes a variety of performance metrics. Core quantitative 
data such as Total Streams and 7-Day Performance illustrate the overall reach of a song, while Stream Change indicates 
the daily growth or decline in listenership. A unique feature of this data is the Viral Score, which measures social 
media engagement and the "shareability" of a track. Additionally, metadata like Genre and Country allow for an analysis 
of how different styles such as K-Pop, Hip Hop, or Pop perform in specific geographic regions.

Categorization and Trend Analysis:
The dataset is highly structured using specific labels to define a song’s current status in the market. Movement is 
tracked via the Trend column (labeled as Rising or Falling). Furthermore, popularity is classified as either Trending 
or Average to help distinguish immediate hits from standard performance.
