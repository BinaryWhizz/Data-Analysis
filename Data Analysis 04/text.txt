Amazon Stock Data 2025

Dataset Description
The Amazon Stock Data 2025 dataset provides comprehensive historical stock market data for Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN), 
covering the period from May 1997 to February 2025. The dataset includes daily trading information such as opening price, 
closing price, highest and lowest prices, adjusted closing price, and trading volume.

Amazon is one of the world’s largest technology and e-commerce companies. As of February 2025, Amazon holds a market 
capitalization of $2.362 trillion USD, making it one of the most valuable companies globally. The dataset reflects 
Amazon’s growth trajectory from its early public trading years to its position as a dominant multinational corporation.

This dataset is suitable for financial analysis, time series forecasting, algorithmic trading research, volatility 
modeling, and machine learning applications.



Covers over 27 years of historical stock data
Daily stock price movements (Open, High, Low, Close)
Adjusted closing prices accounting for stock splits and dividends
Trading volume for liquidity and market activity analysis
Suitable for ARIMA, LSTM, regression, and deep learning models
Ready for visualization and financial analytics
